### Overall Evaluation

1. **Working Environment**

I have a positive view of learning through a real application on AWS. The project creates opportunities to consider source code, infrastructure, access control, and costs together. This approach helps me understand the responsibilities involved in deploying an application to the cloud.

2. **Support from Mentor / Team Admin**

I appreciate the guiding role of Mentors and Team Admins in the learning journey. For questions about IAM, architecture, and AI limitations, I recognize the value of preparing a focused question with relevant configuration and verification results. This is a habit I want to keep developing when seeking technical support.

3. **Relevance of Work to Academic Major**

The project aligns with my Software Engineering major. Frontend, backend, databases, testing, and CI/CD directly relate to my studies. AWS expands that perspective to deployment, security, and product operations.

4. **Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**

The clearest value for me is the opportunity to study a complete deployment flow: Terraform infrastructure, GitHub Actions with OIDC, an ECS backend, and CloudFront web delivery. Bedrock introduces controlled AI integration through a read-only tool, Guardrails, and an evaluation suite. I also learn to revisit technical choices using test results and cost measurements.

5. **Company Culture & Team Spirit**

I value the knowledge-sharing and evidence-based discussion that the program encourages. The project illustrates how a clear guide, a decision record, or a reproducible failure case can help fellow learners. I want to contribute through understandable documentation and questions supported by prior investigation.

6. **Internship Policies / Benefits**

For me, the main value of the internship is the opportunity to learn AWS through a concrete problem and build a product I can explain. I have a positive view of the emphasis on independent learning, practice, and reflection. Specific arrangements and benefits should be checked against official program information.

### Additional Questions

**What has the greatest value for me?**

The opportunity to connect software development with infrastructure, access control, monitoring, and measurable costs. Decisions such as demo-session operation or moving Bedrock calls to another Region show how engineering choices must fit actual constraints.

**Would I continue learning and recommend the program?**

I want to continue studying AWS and would share this learning opportunity with peers interested in cloud computing. I would encourage them to prepare the fundamentals, read the documentation, and verify the outcome of each lab.

### Suggestions & Expectations

I hope the program continues its emphasis on hands-on learning and knowledge sharing. Discussions about architecture, IAM, cost control, and AI evaluation would help learners understand how a real product operates.

For future cohorts, I would welcome milestone checklists and examples of test records, cost reviews, and failure reports. These would help learners track progress and prepare evidence-based reports. Personally, I intend to keep developing initiative, purposeful learning, and clearer records of results.
